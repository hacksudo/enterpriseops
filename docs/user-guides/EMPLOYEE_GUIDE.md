# EnterpriseOps — Employee User Guide

## 1. Starting Your Workday
1. Navigate to the login page and sign in using your employee credentials (e.g. `tester@enterpriseops.internal`).
2. On your **Employee Dashboard** (`/dashboard/employee`), click the prominent emerald **START WORK / I'M ON WORK** button.
3. The platform records your check-in timestamp and activates the live elapsed timer.

---

## 2. Managing Tasks
- **View Assigned Tasks**: Browse tasks delegated to you by your PMO or Team Lead.
- **Create Self-Tasks**: Click **Create Task** to initiate an ad-hoc or self-directed project deliverable.
- **Update Progress**: As you work, move tasks from *Planned* to *In Progress* to track active effort.

---

## 3. Submitting Daily Activity Reports
At the end of your shift or work session:
1. Click **Submit Daily Summary** on your dashboard.
2. Record:
   - What work was performed today.
   - Any blockers or obstacles encountered.
   - Target next steps for tomorrow.
3. Click **Submit Daily Report**. Your structured update is immediately routed to PMO and Department Administrator dashboards.
4. Click **End Work Session** to conclude time tracking for the day.
